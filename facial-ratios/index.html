GLOWD measurement guide

11 facial ratios, explained.

Facial ratios compare one measured distance with another, which makes them less dependent on image size. GLOWD currently surfaces these 11 proportions in its result experience, alongside scores and reference context.

01Middle third
The distance from the brow line to the base of the nose, divided by estimated total face height.
02Eye separation
The distance between the pupil centers, divided by cheekbone width. Eye-corner centers are used if iris points are unavailable.
03Facial index
Estimated face height divided by cheekbone width, measured with pixel-corrected coordinates.
04Top third
The distance from an estimated hairline point to the brow line, divided by estimated total face height.
05Lower third
The distance from the base of the nose to the bottom of the chin, divided by estimated total face height.
06Eyebrow position
The average vertical gap between each brow center and the top of its eye, divided by total face height.
07Jawline definition
Jaw width at selected lower-face points divided by cheekbone width.
08Facial symmetry
The average left-to-right deviation across paired eye, brow, nose, and mouth landmarks, normalized by face width. Lower deviation is more symmetric.
09Nose width
The width between the outer sides of the nose divided by the distance between the inner eye corners.
10Mouth width
The distance between the mouth corners divided by measured nose width.
11Chin-to-philtrum
The vertical length from below the lower lip to the chin divided by the length from the nose base to the upper lip.

How to read them

A ratio is descriptive, not a diagnosis. GLOWD compares each measurement with a configured reference center and shows its contribution to the Harmony Score. Camera angle and expression can change the measurement, so repeat scans are most useful when capture conditions stay similar.
