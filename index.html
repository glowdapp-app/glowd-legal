Common questions

Before your first scan.

What is a GLOWD face score?

The GLOWD Harmony Score summarizes measured facial proportions into an easy-to-read result. It is intended for entertainment and education, not as an objective definition of beauty or a medical assessment.

How does GLOWD analyze a face?

GLOWD maps facial landmarks on the device, calculates proportions between selected points, and compares those measurements with reference distributions. The current results experience explains 11 ratios.

Does GLOWD upload my face photo?

No. Face photos and landmark analysis stay on your device and are not uploaded for facial processing. The app uses limited analytics and subscription services as described in the privacy policy.

Can I improve the consistency of my score?

Use a front-facing photo, neutral expression, even light, and keep your full face visible. Camera angle, expression, hair coverage, and shadows can affect landmark measurements.

Is GLOWD available on Android?

GLOWD is currently available for iPhone and requires iOS 17 or later.
